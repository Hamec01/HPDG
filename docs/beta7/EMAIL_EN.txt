Subject: HPDG Beta 7 - a new 808 engine, ATL Classic and Dark Trap

Hi!

Thank you for supporting HPDG! Beta 7 is ready, and I would love to hear your feedback.

Since Beta 6:
- More distinct ATLClassic and DarkTrap kick, hi-hat and 808 behaviour, a compact bass register and improved sample guidance.
- The HPDG 808 engine in Sub808, with a sound strip/editor, sample layer, filters, drive and presets. Six new ATL/Dark voices bring the library to 919 audio samples.
- Trap Rolls: Off / Style / Fills, with finer grid and triplet editing.
- Sample key estimation from Basic Pitch notes, with a chroma fallback. Your manual key selection still takes priority.
- Fixes for 808 note-end crackle and fewer UI-related audio interruptions.

How to update:
1. Save your project and a backup.
2. Open HPDG and press Update next to the version.
3. Enter the key from your purchase email if prompted; wait for the download and installer verification.
4. Confirm the installer launch and close your DAW before files are replaced. Follow any installer request to close applications.
5. Finish installing, restart your DAW and rescan VST3 if needed. Check that HPDG shows 0.1.0 Beta 7.

Alternatively, obtain the installer at https://hamloprod.org/en/vst with your key and install with the DAW closed. The key is for updates only; making music needs no activation or constant internet connection.

The PDF guide explains the new features, both Trap styles, simple formulas and practical workflows.
