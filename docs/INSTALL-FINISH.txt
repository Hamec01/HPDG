HPDG VST3 is installed.

1. Open your 64-bit DAW and rescan VST3 plugins.
2. Add HPDG to an instrument track.
3. Choose a genre and length, press Generate Pattern, then Play.

Guide: Start menu -> HamloProd -> HPDG FAQ, or click "HPDG" in the plugin
header -> Instruction.
The documentation is also in the HPDG.vst3\Documentation folder.
If Windows asks for a restart, do it before opening your DAW.
No activation is needed. Keep your license key (from your purchase email):
HPDG asks for it once, when you install an update from the plugin.
