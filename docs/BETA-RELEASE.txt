HPDG 0.1.0 Beta 5 — HamloProd
Windows 10/11, x64, VST3 only.

Includes the VST3 plugin, the full library of 913 audio samples, the guide
and the rights notices. No activation and no internet connection are needed
to use the plugin. The Microsoft Visual C++ Runtime is included and installed
if needed.

Close your DAW before installing. The plugin goes into the standard Windows
folder: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\HPDG.vst3.
The samples live inside that bundle, next to the plugin binary.
After installing, rescan VST3 plugins in your DAW.

This is a beta: keep backups of your projects and report bugs with your DAW,
Windows version, genre and the steps that led to the problem.
Standalone is not part of this release.

What's new in Beta 5:
- Update button next to the version in the header. Click it to check for
  a new version: Latest, Update: <version> or Offline.
- In-plugin updates for buyers: enter your license key once (from your
  purchase email) and HPDG downloads the new installer, checks that it is
  complete and unchanged, and starts it when you confirm.
- Click "HPDG" in the header for the menu: Update, Instruction, Site.
- No internet? HPDG works exactly as before.

What's new in Beta 3:
- New genre Atmospheric: Pulse, Two-Step and Broken rhythms, a melodic bass
  over minor harmony and its own drums and basses (64 new samples).
- Intensity 1-5 in the Instrument Rack header: 1 is the simplest beat, 3 is
  as before, 5 is the most hits. The next Generate / Mutate / RG use it.
- Strength knob on every lane (instead of pan): left removes the weakest
  hits, right adds hits typical for the genre.
- Trap: 808 in the right register, bass lines across the whole phrase with
  glides, kick and snare after reference trap packs; the hat Strength knob
  removes whole rolls first and keeps the quarter notes.
- Sub808 plays the HPDG synth sub by default (4 variants, tuned to C2).
- Dragging MIDI / WAV into the DAW: the plugin window hides once the file
  leaves it and comes back after the drop (FL Studio and others).
- Sample Analysis: Guide generation is now the default mode.

What's new in Beta 2:
- Trim sample: Loop / Play / Stop buttons; dragging the edges no longer
  restarts the sound; a click on the waveform sets the play cursor.
- Trim sample: "Selection = N bars" field - choose how many bars the
  selection holds and the tempo follows (for example exactly 4 bars).
- Sub808 plays notes as written; the - / + buttons on the bass lane change
  the octave (Shift - a semitone), and MIDI export keeps it.
- Dark and Steel skins, switched right from the SKIN menu.
- Techno: a second drum set (tech house) and basses, 100 new samples.
